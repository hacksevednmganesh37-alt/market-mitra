# Market Mitra

An Analyst agent finds the opportunities. A Trader agent acts on them. Every trade explained.

Two AI agents talk over BAND to analyse Indian stocks (NSE), US stocks and crypto, make **simulated (paper)
trades** with hard risk rules, and teach beginners the reason behind every decision.

Code: https://github.com/hacksevednmganesh37-alt/market-mitra

Paper trading only. No real money. For learning, not investment advice.
